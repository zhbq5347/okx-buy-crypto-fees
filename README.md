## What “buy crypto” usually means

When people search for “buy crypto,” they usually want to complete one of three tasks:

- Convert U.S. dollars or another local currency into Bitcoin, Ethereum, stablecoins, or another supported asset.
- Find a payment method that is fast and reasonably priced.
- Understand how to buy without accidentally paying more through hidden fees, unfavorable spreads, or unnecessary conversions.

An exchange can handle this in different ways. A simple **Buy** order gives you a quoted purchase price through the platform’s buy flow. A **spot trade** uses an order book, where you select a trading pair and place a market or limit order. **Convert** exchanges one eligible crypto asset for another using a quote.

These options are related, but they are not identical. OKX describes Buy, Sell, Convert, and spot trading as separate functions. The Buy flow uses an available payment method or balance, while spot trading uses an order book and depends on your order type and market liquidity.

That distinction matters because a beginner buying $50 of Bitcoin may care more about a clear checkout quote than about advanced order types. Someone making frequent trades may care more about maker and taker fees.

## Common mistakes when buying crypto

### Ignoring the network

When you withdraw crypto to another wallet, the selected network must match the receiving wallet. Sending an asset through an unsupported network can make recovery difficult or impossible.

### Buying the wrong asset

Check the ticker, network, and asset name before confirming. Crypto platforms may list several assets with similar names or symbols.

### Treating a temporary hold as a failed purchase

A card or ACH payment may be authorized before the final crypto order status is updated. Check the activity page and bank statement before trying again.

### Assuming every feature is available everywhere

OKX states that products and services can differ by region, and some U.S. states or account types may have restrictions. A feature described in a general article may not appear in your account.

### Confusing buying with trading

The Buy screen is usually easier for a one-time purchase. Spot trading gives you more control over order type and price, but it also introduces maker/taker rules, trading pairs, and liquidity considerations.
